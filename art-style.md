# backlot-64 art style

Art for a Commodore 64 game engine. Every image is converted to the VIC-II's real limits after generation, so the closer the source is to those limits, the better it survives.

## Aesthetic
Late-1980s Commodore 64 game art. Chunky pixel art with flat shading, clean one-pixel black outlines, bold readable silhouettes. Vehicle portraits look like a 16-bit era showroom or garage screen. Scenes look like painted C64 loading screens.

## Color palette
Use only the 16 Commodore 64 colours, and nothing else:
black #000000, white #FFFFFF, red #68372B, cyan #70A4B2, purple #6F3D86, green #588D43, blue #352879, yellow #B8C76F, orange #6F4F25, brown #433900, pink #9A6759, dark grey #444444, mid grey #6C6C6C, light green #9AD284, light blue #6C5EB5, light grey #959595.
No gradients. Where a blend is needed, use a visible checkerboard of two of these colours. Large flat areas of a single colour are good.

## Lighting & mood
One hard light from the upper left, hard-edged shadows, no soft shading. Moods are Florida: sunset pastels by day, neon pink and cyan at night on black.

## Rendering technique / medium
Pixel art with large pixels that are twice as wide as they are tall, like C64 multicolour mode. No anti-aliasing, no blur, no texture, no noise, no film grain. Crisp edges. At most four colours in any 8x8 pixel region. Details no thinner than two pixels.

## Composition & framing
The subject is centred and fills about 80% of the frame. For a portrait, a plain single-colour background (black) with a simple dark grey floor. Vehicles are shown in front three-quarter view with the nose to the lower left and both wheels fully visible, round, and correctly proportioned: tyres black, rims grey, wheel arches visible.

## Consistency rules
Black one-pixel outlines on every object. Windows cyan. Highlights white or light grey. Police livery: white body, a blue stripe along the side, the word POLICE in blue on the door, a black roof light bar with a red lamp on the left and a blue lamp on the right.

## Output conventions
PNG, opaque background, no border, no caption, no watermark, no text other than what the prompt asks for.

## Avoid
Photorealism, 3D renders, gradients, soft shadows, reflections, lens flare, motion blur, chrome, more than 16 colours, tiny details, isometric or top-down angles unless asked, cropped wheels, extra text or logos.
